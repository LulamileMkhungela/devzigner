# Business data model
Ask which business concepts, events, owners and data rules matter, proposing a simple default for confirmation. Describe a business glossary, conceptual and logical entities, relevant fields and relationships, cardinality where known, validation and lifecycle rules, ownership, reporting needs, privacy and retention, and external information exchanges. Separate confirmed rules from assumptions and unresolved questions. For an existing product, use only supplied documents or source references; never claim to have discovered a current schema. Avoid prescribing physical database tables, migrations or vendors in a business model. Hand off technical decisions to development or a database specialist.
