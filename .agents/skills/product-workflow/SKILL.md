# Product workflow
Ask clarifying questions with a suggested default before drafting. Work from problem and evidence through audiences, outcomes, scope, feature flows, acceptance criteria, data, dependencies, roadmap and review. Clearly separate confirmed facts, assumptions and open questions. Do not invent research or claim stakeholder approval. Preserve the project's files in a spec folder and hand off decisions and risks.
