About Builder Methods

Stay ahead of how we build.

Builder Methods is where builders stay current on how we build with AI, and compare notes with people doing the same work.

You're not the only one figuring this out.

I've been building software for 20 years as a developer, a designer, and a founder. These days I build with AI the same way you do: directing agents, shipping things people rely on, and watching the craft change under us.

Most of us are working that out alone, with an agent for company. You ship something that works, then quietly wonder whether it's the right way, the best way, or whether you're missing something obvious.

That question is why I started Builder Methods. Workshops show what's actually working across many builders right now. The community is where we compare notes. Fewer things you have to figure out alone.

Brian Casel

Founder, Builder Methods

Who this is for.

Developers, product people, and technical builders from every industry. If you direct agents, ship things people rely on, and intend to stay at the front of the craft, you belong here.

  • You already build

    You direct coding agents, write skills and instructions, and ship apps, tools, agent skills, or workflows that others depend on.

  • You're energized by it

    There's always something new to try. Exploring what's now possible is part of the fun of the work.

  • You want it from the front lines

    From peers who build every day, not from beginner tutorials or commentators.
