Frequently asked questions.

What's included with Pro?

Every live workshop, the full recording library with summaries and transcripts, the private community chat, and direct access to Brian and other pro builders. All courses and Build Kits are included too.

How much experience do I need?

You don't need to be a professional developer or have shipped a finished product. Pro welcomes advanced beginners, intermediate builders, and experienced practitioners. The courses help you build foundations, while workshops and the community help you explore new methods and apply them to your own work. Check each workshop's description for its topic and focus.

I'm new to building with AI. How can I learn?

Start with our courses to learn essential skills and workflows at your own pace, then put them into practice on something you want to build. Brian and the community are here to answer questions, help you work through roadblocks, and offer feedback along the way.

What if I can't attend live workshops?

All workshop recordings are always available to Pro members, with written summaries and transcripts so you can catch up on your schedule. You also have our full course library and access to the community to keep learning, ask questions, and get support whenever you need it.

Do I need a particular AI tool or tech stack?

No. Workshops cover different tools and approaches across code, agents, product, design, and other building work. Some focus on a specific tool, while others cover methods you can use across stacks. The workshop description tells you what we'll be working with.

Can I join with my team?

Yes. Choose "For my team" in pricing and set your seat count. Each person gets a full membership, so you can learn the same methods and put them to work together. Manage seats and billing from one account. For private team training or a custom workshop, get in touch.

Can my company pay?

Yes. Many members expense Pro through their company. Receipts and invoices are available from your account.

What if Pro isn't right for me?

If you decide within 14 days of joining that Pro isn't for you, request immediate cancellation and a full refund.

Is Builder Methods independent?

Yes. Builder Methods isn't owned by an AI company or tool vendor. Brian and the community share what works in their own builds.
