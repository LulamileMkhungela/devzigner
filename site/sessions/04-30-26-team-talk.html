Talking AI Adoption for Teams

Real talk for teams adopting AI — challenges, strategies, and what's working.

Spring ’26

Join Pro to watch

If you're leading a team or working on one, this session is for you. We'll dig into the real challenges of adopting AI-powered building workflows across a team — what's actually working, where the resistance shows up, and how to move things forward without burning political capital or slowing down delivery.

This isn't a theoretical discussion about "the future of teams." It's a working session where team leaders and team members share what they're navigating right now — tooling decisions, process changes, getting buy-in, upskilling, and everything in between.

What to expect:

  • Open discussion on team adoption strategies and challenges
  • Real stories from leaders and team members in the trenches
  • Practical advice on buy-in, tooling, process, and culture

Members of all experience levels are welcome — whether you're a team lead trying to introduce new workflows, a developer navigating changes on your team, or a founder figuring out how to work with contractors and collaborators.

Submit your request to be included. No need to prepare a deck — just bring your situation, your challenges, and your questions.
