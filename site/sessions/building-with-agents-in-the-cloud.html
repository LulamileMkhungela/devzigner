Building with agents in the cloud

A live workshop on moving your projects to the cloud, organizing your repos, and orchestrating multiple agents.

Fall ’26

Get the recording · $50

Pro members attend free. Log in

Over the past month, I've moved from working on projects locally to working with agents in cloud environments. It's been such a better way to build—and I'm convinced cloud-based workflows are where we're all headed in the coming months.

In this live workshop, I'll walk you through how I'm working today and how to make the transition yourself.

What we'll cover

  • Why move to the cloud? The benefits of cloud-based development compared with working locally.

  • Tools for cloud-based work. Working with agents and repositories in cloud environments.

  • Organizing your projects. Using GitHub repos to keep your projects and files organized and accessible to your agents.

  • Running multiple agents at once. Multitasking and orchestrating work across cloud projects instead of managing local Git worktrees.

  • Cloud-based bots. Where tools like Grok Bot fit into this workflow.

This is a practical look at the setup I'm using every day: what's changed, what's working, and how you can start moving your own projects to the cloud.
